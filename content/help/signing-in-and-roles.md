## Signing in

Sign in with your company email address and the password an admin gave you. If you have no password yet or forgot it, ask an admin to set a new one.

## Your first sign-in

A password an admin gives you is temporary. After you sign in with it, you land on a **Choose a new password** page:

1. Type the password you were given in **Temporary password**.
2. Type a new password of at least 10 characters in **New password**, then type it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Choose **Change password**. You are signed out; sign in again with your new password.

Until you do this, every page sends you back to this screen. You can use **Sign out** if you need to leave without changing it. If an admin resets your password later, you will be asked to choose a new one again.

## If you cannot sign in

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an admin to set a new password, which also lifts the lock.

To change your password at any time, select your profile picture at the top right, choose **Account settings** and use **Password**. You are signed out afterwards; sign in again with the new password.

If you still cannot sign in, ask an admin to check that your account is active and that your email is on the list of allowed addresses.

## What your role can do

Every account has one role. Each role includes everything the role above it can do.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Requester** | Create requests. See your own KPI. |
| **Creative** | Everything above, plus move requests across the board and manage projects. |
| **Lead** | Everything above, plus assign requests to people and see Team KPI. |
| **Admin** | Everything above, plus manage users and lists. |

If a menu item or button is missing, your role does not include that action. Ask an admin if you think your role should have it.
