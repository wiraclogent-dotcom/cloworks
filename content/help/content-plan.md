**Content Plan** is where social media posts are planned before they become creative requests. Each brand channel has a grid that looks like its Instagram profile, so you can see how upcoming posts will sit next to the ones already published.

## Who can do what

- **Everyone** can open Content Plan, view channels and posts, and comment on a post at any time.
- **Creatives, leads, admins and people with the Social Media job role** can draft posts and submit them.
- **Leads and admins** can send a post back, approve it, and manage channels.

## View a channel

1. Open **Content Plan** from the sidebar.
2. Pick a brand channel under **Channels**.
3. Use the tabs to switch between the **Feed**, **Reels** and **Stories** views. Select a post to open it in a side panel with a phone preview.

## Draft a post

1. Open the **Feed**, **Reels** or **Stories** tab you are planning for, then select the **+** tile at the top of the grid. Clowork adds a new post as an **Idea** in that tab and opens it in the side panel.
2. Add media: a mock-up or reference. Images can be up to 10 MB. Videos can be up to 100 MB and 90 seconds. Use JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, MP4 or MOV.
3. Write the **Brief**. This is the message for the creative team.
4. Write the **Caption draft** if you have one.
5. Pick a **Post date**.

If **Media storage is not configured**, uploads are turned off. Ask an admin to set it up.

You can edit a post while it is an idea, a draft, or sent back with changes requested. Once it is submitted or approved, it is locked. You can still comment.

### Date warning

A post shows a ⚠ **Date out of order** warning when its date does not fit the grid order. For example, a post sits above a post with an earlier date. To fix it, open the post and select **Set to** *date* in the warning to take a date that fits, change the post date yourself, or drag the post to the right place.

### Reorder by dragging

Drag a planned post to a new spot in the grid. The order of your planned posts changes. Posts that are already locked stay where they are.

## Submit for approval

A post needs a **Brief** and a **Post date** before you can select **Submit for approval**. After you submit, a lead or admin reviews it.

## Send back or approve

A lead or admin opens a submitted post and chooses one of two actions.

- **Send back**: write a **Feedback note** (required). The author gets the post back with the status **Changes requested**, edits it, and submits again.
- **Approve & create request**: check the title, type, division, deadline, outputs and assignee, then confirm. Clowork creates a normal request. The post's author is the requester, and the mock-ups are attached to the request. The deadline starts 3 days before the post date, and never earlier than today.

## Follow the request

After approval, the post follows its request. When the request moves to **On progress**, the post shows **In production**. When the request is **Done**, the post shows **Done**. You do not update the post yourself. While the post is **In production** or **Done**, leads, admins, people who can draft posts, and any of the request's assignees can add the finished design under **Final files** in the post. If the request is cancelled, the post goes back to **Draft**, so you can edit it and submit it again. Open the linked request from the post to see details. See [Request details and statuses](/help/request-details-and-statuses).

## Already posted tiles

Use **Already posted** to add tiles for posts that are already live, so the grid matches the real profile. These tiles take a media file and a **Posted on** date. They do not create a request.

## Manage channels

Leads and admins manage channels from the **Channels** list.

1. Select **Add channel**.
2. Choose the **Brand**, then enter a **Display name** and **Instagram handle**. Add an **Avatar** if you like.
3. To change a channel, use the manage (gear) button on a channel: **Edit**, **Move up**, **Move down**, or **Delete**.
4. To remove an avatar, select **Remove avatar**.
