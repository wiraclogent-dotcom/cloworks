Clowork tracks creative requests from the moment someone asks for a piece of work until it is done.

## What you will find

- **Requests** is where the work lives. See the board, table, and calendar views.
- **Projects** groups related requests together.
- **My KPI** shows your own output and on-time numbers for the month.
- **Team KPI** shows the same numbers for the whole team, if your role can see them.

## Your first steps

1. Sign in with your company email address. See [Signing in and roles](/help/signing-in-and-roles).
2. Open **Requests** from the sidebar. Selecting the Clowork logo at the top of the sidebar always brings you back here.
3. Select a card to open its details, or select **New request** to ask for new work. See [Creating a request](/help/creating-a-request).

The sidebar shows only the sections your role can use.

## Your profile menu

Your profile card sits at the bottom left of the sidebar: your photo, name, title and department. Select it to open **Settings**, the **Help center**, switch **Dark mode** on or off, or **Sign out**. The bell at the top right of every page holds your notifications. Admins also find **Members**, **Lists** and **Marketing calendar** as tabs in **Settings**.
