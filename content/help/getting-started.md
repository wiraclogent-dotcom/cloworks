Cloworks tracks creative requests from the moment someone asks for a piece of work until it is done.

## What you will find

- **Requests** is where the work lives. See the board, table, and calendar views.
- **Projects** groups related requests together.
- **My KPI** shows your own output and on-time numbers for the month.
- **Team KPI** shows the same numbers for the whole team, if your role can see them.
- **Admin** holds user and list settings, if your role can manage them.

## Your first steps

1. Sign in with your company email address. See [Signing in and roles](/help/signing-in-and-roles).
2. Open **Requests** from the sidebar.
3. Select a card to open its details, or select **New request** to ask for new work. See [Creating a request](/help/creating-a-request).

The sidebar shows only the sections your role can use.
