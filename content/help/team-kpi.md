**Team KPI** shows the same numbers as [My KPI](/help/my-kpi) for every member of your team: the creative team (the designers), or the social media team if your job role is Social media. Only leads and admins can open it.

## Using Team KPI

1. Open **Team KPI** from the sidebar.
2. Use the month picker to choose the month.
3. Select a person to open their KPI page.

## Shared requests

A request can have several assignees. Each of them earns the full point, so a shared request shows as one task done in every assignee's row. The team total under **Request tasks done** counts each request once, even when several people worked on it, so it can be lower than the sum of the rows.

If no team members are listed, no one with your team's job role has been added yet. Ask an admin to check the people list.

## Social media team

People with the job role **Social media** are measured in **Content Plan** posts: **Posts approved** counts the posts each person wrote that a lead approved that month, and **On-time** is the share approved on or before the post's planned date. Targets are posts per month. Admins can switch between **Creative** and **Social media** at the top of the page; the download follows the team you are looking at.
